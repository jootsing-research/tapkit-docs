The TapKit plugin adds the TapKit connector and TapKit's [skills](/integrations/skills) to Claude Chat and Cowork. Install it from Cowork in the Claude app. For the terminal, see [Claude Code](/integrations/claude-code).

## Step 1: Add the TapKit plugin marketplace

1. Open **Cowork** in the Claude app.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Customize**.
3. Go to **Personal Plugins** and click the **+** button.
4. You'll see a popup with **Browse Plugins** and **Create Plugin** — hover over **Create Plugin**.
5. Click **Add Marketplace**.
6. In the popup, paste the following:

```
jootsing-research/tapkit-plugins-claude
```

## Step 2: Install the TapKit plugin

1. Still in Cowork under **Customize**, click **Browse Plugins**.
2. Find the **TapKit** plugin in the list.
3. Click **Install**, then click **Manage**.

You should now see TapKit listed under **Personal Plugins** in the Customize sidebar.

## Step 3: Give it permission

1. In the Customize sidebar, click on the **TapKit** plugin.
2. Click on **Connectors**, then select the **TapKit** connector.
3. On the right side next to the tools, set the permission to **Always allow**.

The first time Claude uses TapKit, it asks you to sign in to TapKit in your browser. Click **Allow access** on the consent screen.

## Try it

- "What's on my phone right now?"
- "Add eggs to my Groceries list in Reminders."
- "Set a timer on my iPhone for two minutes."

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Claude says TapKit isn't connected">
    Open the TapKit connector under **Customize** and reconnect it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Claude can't find a phone, or the phone is offline">
    Make sure the Mac app is open and signed in, and the phone's tile isn't showing a setup message. Ask Claude to "list my phones" to see what TapKit sees.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Actions time out">
    Unlock the phone and check that it's still plugged in. If the phone's tile in the Mac app shows a problem, fix that first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
