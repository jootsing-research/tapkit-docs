## What you need

- A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 14.6 or later, with Bluetooth
- An iPhone and a USB cable that carries data
- A Google account to sign in to TapKit

## 1. Install TapKit and connect your iPhone

<a href="https://github.com/Jootsing-Research/tapkit-releases/releases/latest/download/TapKit.dmg" download>Download TapKit</a>, drag it into **Applications**, and open it. The app walks you through signing in, granting Camera and Bluetooth access, trusting the Mac on your iPhone, and pairing over Bluetooth.

## 2. Run a task in the Mac app

Click the chat button on your phone in the Mac app and type a task in the **Do anything** box:

> Open the Clock app and start a 2 minute timer

The agent takes screenshots, taps, and types on the phone while you watch.

## 3. Connect your own AI tools

Install the TapKit plugin for Claude or Codex, or add the TapKit MCP server to any MCP client.
