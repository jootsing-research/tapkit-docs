Paste any of these into the Mac app's agent chat, or into Claude or Codex with the TapKit plugin installed.

## Everyday tasks

> Add eggs to my Groceries list in Reminders.

> When is my next meeting with Bob? Check my calendar.

> Set a timer for two minutes, check that it's running, then cancel it.

> Check my battery health and tell me the maximum capacity.

> Find the note called Packing List and summarize it.

## Testing apps

Write the test as a script: each step, and what should be on screen after it.

> Open Safari and go to example.com. Check that the page shows the heading "Example Domain". Tap "More information...". Check that a new page loads, and report whether each step passed.

## Writing better prompts

- **Name the app.** "In Reminders" is faster than letting the agent guess.
- **Be specific.** Include dates, names, and amounts rather than "find a cheap one".
- **Say where to stop.** Tell the agent whether it may send, post, or pay, or should stop and check with you first.
- **Sign in first.** Agents can't get past Face ID or CAPTCHAs, and won't type a password you haven't given them.
- **Mind the typing limits.** Each typing action takes up to 100 US-ASCII characters. Emoji and accented characters can't be typed.
