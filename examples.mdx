Paste any of these into the Mac app's agent chat, or into Claude or Codex with the TapKit plugin installed.

## Everyday tasks

> Add eggs to my Groceries list in Reminders.

> When is my next meeting with Bob? Check my calendar.

> Set a timer for two minutes, check that it's running, then cancel it.

> Check my battery health and tell me the maximum capacity.

> Find the note called Packing List and summarize it.

## Testing apps

Write a test as a list of steps, with what should be on screen after each one.

> Open Safari and go to example.com. Check that the page shows the heading "Example Domain". Tap "More information...". Check that a new page loads, and report whether each step passed.

## Writing better prompts

Name the app you want the agent to use, so it doesn't have to guess. Give specific dates, names, and amounts instead of asking for "a cheap one". Say whether the agent may send, post, or pay, or should check with you first.

Sign in to apps before you hand them to an agent. Agents can't get past Face ID or CAPTCHAs, and won't type a password you haven't given them. Agents also can't type emoji or accented letters, because each typing action takes up to 100 US-ASCII characters.
