The TapKit Mac app hosts your iPhones. It must be open and signed in for any agent — built-in or external — to use them.

## Phone grid

Every connected iPhone gets a tile showing its live screen. Each tile has:

- **Chat** — opens the agent chat for that phone.
- **Mute** — the phone's audio plays through the Mac. It starts muted.
- **Home** — goes to the Home Screen.
- **More** — **Turn Off AssistiveTouch**, **Reset AssistiveTouch**, and **Force Disconnect**.

If a phone isn't ready, its tile says why and what to do — for example **Unlock Your iPhone** or **Tap Trust on Your iPhone**. See [Setup troubleshooting](/setup#troubleshooting).

### Controlling a phone by hand

Pick a gesture from the menu in the bottom-left corner, then click on the phone's screen:

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| Live | Click and drag directly, like using a finger |
| Tap, Double Tap, Triple Tap | Tap at the clicked point |
| Tap & Hold | Long press, for context menus |
| Flick | Quick swipe in a direction, for scrolling and paging |
| Drag | Press, move, and release at a chosen speed |
| Hold & Drag | Long press, then drag, for moving icons and reordering lists |

Your Mac's keyboard isn't forwarded to the phone.

## Agent chat

The chat sidebar runs TapKit's built-in agent. Click **Chat** on a phone's tile, type a task in **Do anything**, and press Return:

> Add eggs to my Groceries list in Reminders

The agent takes screenshots, taps, flicks, types, and presses keys until the task is done, and asks you when it needs something. Each step shows up in the chat as it happens. Click **Stop** to interrupt it, or **New chat** to start over.

The agent can see every connected phone and will ask which one to use if it's not clear.

<Note>
  Chats aren't saved when you quit TapKit.
</Note>

## Settings

Click the **Account** button in the toolbar to open settings.

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Preferences | Defaults for new agent chats: **Preview guidance** (whether the agent checks each tap location before tapping) and **Coordinates** (image pixels or 0–1000). |
| Profile | The email you're signed in with. |
| Phones | Every phone TapKit has seen. Rename a phone here — the name is what agents see. |
| Permissions | Camera and Bluetooth access. |
| Connectors | The MCP server URL for connecting other AI tools. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview). |
| Billing | Subscription status, phones included and in use, and adding phones. |

**Sign Out** is at the bottom of the settings sidebar.

## Limits

- The phone must stay plugged in and unlocked. TapKit can't lock or unlock it.
- Each Mac can live-stream one phone to the [web app](/web-app) at a time.
