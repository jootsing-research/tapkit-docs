The TapKit Mac app connects your iPhones to your TapKit account and makes them available to its built-in agent, Claude, Codex, the API, and any other MCP client. The app must be open and signed in for TapKit to see or control a phone.

<img src="/images/mac-app-overview.webp" alt="TapKit Mac app with a connected iPhone on the left and the agent chat on the right" />

## Connected phones

The main window shows every iPhone connected to this Mac, each with its live screen. Plug in another iPhone to add it; it walks through the same trust, screen, and Bluetooth checks as the first one. See [Setup](/setup#adding-more-phones).

Each phone must stay plugged in over USB, unlocked, and paired with the Mac over Bluetooth. If a phone isn't ready, the app says why and what to do.

## Gestures & device controls

You can control a phone by hand from the Mac app: pick a gesture — tap, double or triple tap, tap and hold, flick, drag, or hold and drag — and click on the phone's screen, or use **Live** to click and drag as if with a finger. **Home** goes to the Home Screen.

Manual control is useful for testing the connection, getting a phone into position before a task, or stepping in while an agent is working. Your Mac's keyboard isn't forwarded to the phone.

## Agent chat

The built-in agent is the fastest way to try TapKit without setting up another tool. Open a phone's chat, and type a task:

> Add eggs to my Groceries list in Reminders

The agent looks at the screen, acts, and repeats until the task is done or it needs your input. You can watch each step on the phone and in the chat, and stop it at any time. It can use any connected phone and asks which one if it's not clear.

Chats aren't saved when you quit TapKit.

## Connection status

When a phone isn't ready:

1. Make sure TapKit is open and signed in.
2. Make sure the iPhone is plugged in and unlocked.
3. Follow the message on the phone in the Mac app — for example, tap **Trust** on the iPhone, or reconnect Bluetooth.
4. Make sure the phone is active on your subscription in the [web app](/web-app#phones).

See [Setup troubleshooting](/setup#troubleshooting) for specific problems.

## Settings

Click **Account** in the toolbar to manage:

- **Phones:** rename phones. Agents see these names.
- **Permissions:** review Camera and Bluetooth access.
- **Agent defaults:** how the built-in agent works in new chats.
- **Connectors:** the MCP server URL for connecting other AI tools. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview).
- **Account and billing:** your sign-in, subscription, and how many phones it includes. **Sign Out** is at the bottom.

To run the guided setup again, choose **TapKit > Settings...** (⌘,) and click **Run Setup Again**.

TapKit updates itself. Choose **TapKit > Check for Updates...** to check now.
