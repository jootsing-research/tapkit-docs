The TapKit Mac app connects your iPhones to your TapKit account. Once a phone is connected, the built-in agent, Claude, Codex, the API, and any other MCP client can use it. TapKit can only see or control a phone while the app is open and signed in.

## Connected phones

The main window shows the live screen of every iPhone connected to this Mac. To add a phone, plug it in. It goes through the same trust, screen, and Bluetooth checks as the first one. See [Setup](/setup#adding-more-phones).

Each phone has to stay plugged in, unlocked, and paired with the Mac over Bluetooth. If a phone isn't ready, the app tells you why and what to do.

## Gestures and device controls

You can control a phone by hand from the Mac app. Pick a gesture and click on the phone's screen. The gestures are tap, double tap, triple tap, tap and hold, flick, drag, and hold and drag. **Live** lets you click and drag on the screen like a finger. **Home** goes to the Home Screen.

Use manual control to test the connection, set up a phone before a task, or take over while an agent is working. Your Mac's keyboard doesn't type on the phone.

## Agent chat

The built-in agent lets you try TapKit without setting up another tool. Open a phone's chat and type a task:

> Add eggs to my Groceries list in Reminders

The agent looks at the screen, acts, and repeats until the task is done or it needs your input. Each step shows on the phone and in the chat, and you can stop the agent at any time. The agent can use any connected phone, and asks which one if your task doesn't say.

TapKit doesn't save chats when you quit.

## Connection status

When a phone isn't ready:

1. Check that TapKit is open and signed in.
2. Check that the iPhone is plugged in and unlocked.
3. Follow the message the Mac app shows for that phone. For example, it may ask you to tap **Trust** on the iPhone or reconnect Bluetooth.
4. Check that the phone is active on your subscription in the [web app](/web-app#phones).

[Setup troubleshooting](/setup#troubleshooting) covers specific problems.

## Settings

Most settings are behind the **Account** button in the toolbar. There you can rename a phone, which changes the name agents see. You can also fix Camera or Bluetooth access, copy the MCP server URL to [connect another AI tool](/integrations/overview), manage your subscription, add phones to it, and sign out.

If a phone gets stuck in a state you can't fix, choose **TapKit > Settings...** (⌘,) and click **Run Setup Again** to go through setup from the start.

TapKit updates itself. Choose **TapKit > Check for Updates...** to check now.
