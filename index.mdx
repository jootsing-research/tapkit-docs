TapKit lets AI agents use real iPhones. Plug an iPhone into a Mac running the TapKit app, and an agent can take screenshots of it, tap, and type. The agent can be TapKit's built-in agent, Claude, Codex, or your own code.

Nothing is installed on the iPhone. You don't need to jailbreak it, turn on developer mode, or use a simulator.
